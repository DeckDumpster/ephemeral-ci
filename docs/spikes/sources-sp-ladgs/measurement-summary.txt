CONCLUSION
  16 vCPU is fastest on the mean AND the only reproducible size. 8 vCPU cannot fill the
  slots the pipeline could use; 32 vCPU inflates total work 14-38% through suite
  interference. Recommended EC2 shape for spill: c7i.4xlarge (16 vCPU / 32 GiB), spot.
